# Reviewer Suggestions for Target Journals

When submitting to high-impact journals, you will often be asked to suggest 3-5 potential reviewers. A good suggested reviewer is an expert in your specific methodology (GEE, Sentinel-5P) or region (South Asia, Himalayas) but has no recent collaborative ties with you.

> [!TIP]
> Do **not** suggest people from your own institution or recent co-authors.

### Strategy for Finding Reviewers

Since this paper combines **Remote Sensing (Sentinel-5P)**, **Advanced Statistics (Binary Segmentation, STL, Wavelets, Granger Causality)**, and **Regional Focus (Nepal/Himalayas)**, aim for a mix of these profiles.

---

## Profile 1: The Remote Sensing / S5P Expert
Search for authors who frequently publish on TROPOMI data validation or application in Asia.

**Suggested Search Terms (Google Scholar):**
*   `"Sentinel-5P" AND "Google Earth Engine" AND ("NO2" OR "HCHO")`
*   `"TROPOMI" AND "air quality" AND "trend analysis"`

*Example Profiles:*
*   Researchers who have published global or pan-Asian Sentinel-5P studies in *Remote Sensing of Environment* or *Science of the Total Environment*.

## Profile 2: The Regional Air Quality Expert
Search for authors who have published on air pollution in the Hindu Kush Himalayan (HKH) region, Indo-Gangetic Plain (IGP), or specifically Nepal, but perhaps using different datasets (e.g., MODIS, ground sensors).

**Suggested Search Terms:**
*   `"air pollution" AND "Nepal" AND "spatiotemporal"`
*   `"Kathmandu Valley" AND "air quality" AND "meteorology"`
*   `"transboundary air pollution" AND "Himalayas"`

*Example Profiles:*
*   Authors from ICIMOD (International Centre for Integrated Mountain Development) who haven't co-authored with you.
*   Researchers leading studies on IGP pollution transport into the Himalayas.

## Profile 3: The Statistical / Timeseries Analysis Expert
Search for authors who specialize in the advanced methods you used (Binary Segmentation, STL, Wavelet Coherence, Granger Causality in environmental contexts).

**Suggested Search Terms:**
*   `"Binary Segmentation" AND "time series" AND "air quality"`
*   `"Structural breakpoint" AND "air pollution"`
*   `"Wavelet Transform Coherence" AND "meteorology" AND "pollutants"`
*   `"Granger Causality" AND "air quality"`

*Example Profiles:*
*   Researchers who have applied advanced time series decomposition and causality tests to environmental datasets (often publishing in *Science of the Total Environment* or *Atmospheric Environment*).

---

## Information Needed for Submission

For each suggested reviewer, you will typically need to provide:
1.  **Full Name**
2.  **Institution / Affiliation**
3.  **Institutional Email Address** (avoid Gmail/Yahoo if possible)
4.  **Reason for Suggestion:** (e.g., "Dr. X is an expert in satellite-based air quality monitoring in South Asia and recently published a relevant methodology paper using Google Earth Engine.")
